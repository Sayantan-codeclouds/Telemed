# TeleMed AI
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

---

# 1. Overview

TeleMed AI is an AI-powered telemedicine platform that enables patients to connect with healthcare providers through online consultations, AI-assisted healthcare services, pharmacy ordering, and medical record management.

The platform is designed to provide a complete digital healthcare ecosystem.

---

# 2. Vision

To build an intelligent healthcare platform that combines AI, telemedicine, pharmacy, and patient management into one seamless experience.

---

# 3. Goals

- Easy appointment booking
- AI-powered patient assistance
- Video consultations
- Digital prescriptions
- Pharmacy integration
- Medical records management
- Real-time communication
- Secure healthcare platform
